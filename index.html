About Jade

A counselor who's been on both sides of the process

  • Independent Education Consultant since 2024
  • B.A. Symbolic Systems, Stanford University
  • Four-year Division I athlete, Stanford Women's Crew
  • Trained life coach, Lumina Life Coach Essentials

Jade Thornton is an independent college admissions counselor based in Denver, Colorado, working with students across the country. She holds a B.A. in Symbolic Systems from Stanford University, where she concentrated in cognitive science, rowed for four years with Stanford Women's Crew, and volunteered with Girls Who Code teaching coding fundamentals to low-income students.

Since 2024, Jade has guided high school students individually through every stage of admissions — building a personalized college list, developing application strategy, and drafting essays that sound like the student, not a template. She's currently enrolled in UCLA's College Counseling Certification Program and is a Provisional Member of the Higher Education Consultants Association (HECA).

Her background in cognitive science and life coaching shapes how she works: helping students get clear on what they actually want before worrying about what looks good on paper.
