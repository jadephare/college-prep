Jade Thornton is an Independent Educational Consultant (IEC) based in Denver, Colorado, working with students across the country. She holds a B.S. in Symbolic Systems from Stanford University (see if you can find Jade on the Symbolic Systems major page), where she concentrated in cognitive science. After graduating, Jade worked as a software engineer.

Jade enjoys supporting low-income and first-generation students through organizations like PeerForward. She is currently enrolled in UCLA's College Counseling Certification Program and is expected to complete the program in December.

When she's not working with students or attending a webinar to stay up to date on all things related to college applications, you can find Jade backpacking, hiking up a 14er, or dancing to music in her living room.

Her background in cognitive science and computer engineering shapes how she works: helping students get clear on what they actually want before worrying about what looks good on paper.
